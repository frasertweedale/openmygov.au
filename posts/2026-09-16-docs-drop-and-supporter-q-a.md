# Case documents drop + supporter Q&A this Friday

I have just published a bunch of "meaty" documents from my FOI case.
Unfortunately I can't publish everything I have at this stage.  And
of course, I don't have everything, either, because of s 70
confidentiality orders.

Before I outline what I've published, a quick note that I am doing a
**supporter Q&A this Friday** 18 September at 19:00 UTC+10.  I'll
send the link to my Chuffed and Pozible supporters on Thursday
evening.  If you want to become a supporter, head over to my
[Chuffed campaign](https://chuffed.org/project/mygov-app-source-code-foi-review){target=_blank}.

So, what's in the drop?

In respect of Services Australia's case, I have published:

- The [open affidavit of Mr Garrett McDonald][open]{target=_blank},
  CISO and General Manager Cyber Security at Services Australia
- A redacted version of the [confidential affidavit of Mr
  McDonald][closed]{target=_blank}
- A [further open affidvit of Mr McDonald][reply]{target=_blank}

I am not able to publish Service's Australia *Statement of Facts,
Issues, and Contentions (SOFIC/SFIC)* at this time.  Nor other
submissions that are technically not part of the evidence.  After
the Tribunal hands down their decision, I will apply for these
documents to be released from the *Harman obligation* that prevents
their disclosure.

In respect of my case, I have published:

- [Professor Vanessa Teague's expert report][Teague]{target=_blank}.
  Vanessa analysed the app and gave evidence on its behaviour and
  implementation, and expert opinion about source code and software
  security.
- [Dr Peter Serwylo's expert report][Serwylo]{target=_blank}.  Peter
  gave evidence about Android app development, distribution and
  security.
- [My affidavit][Tweedale]{target=_blank} in which I give evidence
  on a range of topics including open source software development,
  source code transparency policy/regulation/legislation in
  Australia and abroad, and existing efforts to analyse and reverse
  engineer of the App.
- [My *Statement of Facts, Issues and
  Contentions*][ASFIC]{target=_blank}

Finally, I have also published the [*Joint bundle of
authorities*][authorities]{target=_blank} which contains
legislation, case law and other binding instruments relied upon by
either party in this proceeding.
